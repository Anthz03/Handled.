# MASTER PROMPT: BUILD A COMPLETE TECHNOLOGY CONSULTING & DIGITAL SOLUTIONS COMPANY WEBSITE

Act as a Senior UI/UX Designer, Brand Strategist, Full-Stack Developer, Software Architect, Database Engineer, and Digital Marketing Consultant.

Your task is to conceptualize, design, and develop a complete, professional, modern, responsive, and fully functional website for a startup technology consulting company founded by two co-founders.

The website must establish the company as a credible technology consulting and digital solutions provider, showcase its capabilities, introduce its founders, display demonstration projects, and allow prospective clients to submit project inquiries through a functional online form.

Do not create a generic freelancer portfolio. The objective is to create a website that looks and functions like a professional technology consulting agency.

Options:

"I have read and understood the Privacy Notice and consent to the collection and processing of my personal information for the purpose of evaluating my project inquiry, communicating with me, and providing the requested consulting or development services, in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173)."

FORM SUBMISSION FUNCTIONALITY:

Upon successful submission:

1. Validate all required information.
2. Generate a unique inquiry reference number.
3. Save the inquiry to the database.
4. Send an automated acknowledgment email to the client.
5. Send an internal notification to the company.
6. Display a confirmation page.
7. Show the inquiry reference number.
8. Indicate the expected response timeframe of 1–2 business days.
9. Prevent duplicate submissions.
10. Securely process uploaded files.
